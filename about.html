Understanding the Feedback System

Red (Incorrect): The stone is placed where it shouldn't be. Remove it and try elsewhere.

Green (Correct): You're covering the right tiles, but the shape is in the wrong position. Try moving it around or using other shapes to fill the tiles.

Gold (Perfect): This shape is in the EXACT correct position! Don't touch it.

Tips & Tricks

Start with the Biggest Shapes First

Since they have less valid positions, making them easier to place correctly. Save the smaller pieces for last because they pretty much fit everywhere.

Use Gold Pieces as Anchors

Once you get a gold piece, don’t touch it! Try to build around it using the other shapes.

Reposition Instead of Removing

Instead of dragging shapes back into the catalogue, just reposition it in the grid itself. It saves time.

Work One Piece at a Time

Don’t place all the pieces in the grid all at once! Work them one at a time.

Make Use of the Unlimited Moves

Use the larger pieces to scan the grid to give you a rough idea on what the pattern is. There is no move limit.

If All Else Fails

Just spam the piece all over the grid until you get the correct position.
